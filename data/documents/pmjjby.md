# PMJJBY (Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana)

**Category:** Insurance / Social Security  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Finance  
**Official source:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmjjby

## Objective
To provide affordable life insurance coverage to citizens, ensuring financial security for families in case of the policyholder’s death.

## Benefits
- Life insurance cover of **₹2 lakh** in case of death (natural or accidental).  
- Annual premium: **₹436 per member** (auto-debited from bank account).  
- Coverage period: **1 year**, renewable annually (June 1 to May 31).  
- Simple enrollment through banks and insurance companies.  

## Eligibility
- Age group: **18–50 years**.  
- Must have a **savings bank account**.  
- Consent for auto-debit of premium required.  

## Exclusions
- Individuals above 50 years of age at entry.  
- Non-renewal if premium not paid or account inactive.  
- No maturity benefit (pure term insurance).  

## How to Apply
- Approach your **bank branch** or enroll via net banking.  
- Fill consent form and provide Aadhaar/identity proof.  
- Premium auto-debited annually from linked bank account.  
- Coverage continues as long as premium is paid.  

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- If a user mentions being **18–50 years old with a bank account**, PMJJBY “appears relevant.”  
- Always highlight **₹2 lakh cover at ₹436/year**.  
- Clarify exclusions (above 50 years, inactive accounts, no maturity benefit).  
- Stress that PMJJBY is a **pure life insurance scheme**, not a savings or investment plan.
