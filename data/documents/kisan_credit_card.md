# Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

**Category:** Agriculture / Financial Assistance
**Ministry:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare
**Official source:** https://dahd.gov.in/en/division/kcc (or issuing bank's KCC page)

## Objective
Provides farmers with adequate and timely credit, under a single window, for cultivation, post-harvest expenses, farm asset maintenance, and allied agricultural investment needs.

## Benefits
- Short-term credit for crop cultivation, post-harvest expenses, produce marketing, household consumption, and farm asset maintenance.
- Term loans for investments like land development, minor irrigation, and farm equipment.
- Subsidized interest: Government provides 2% interest subvention plus a 3% Prompt Repayment Incentive, bringing the effective rate down to around 4% per annum for prompt repayers.
- Credit limit is fixed based on the scale of finance for the farmer's crop(s) and cultivated area, with the limit increasing ~10% each year over a 5-year card tenure.
- Card can be used via ATMs/micro-ATMs, Business Correspondents, POS at input dealers, and mobile banking.

## Eligibility
- Farmers (individual or joint) who are owner-cultivators.
- Tenant farmers, oral lessees, and share croppers.
- Self Help Groups (SHGs) or Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) of farmers, including tenant farmers and share croppers.

## How to Apply
**Online:** Visit the issuing bank's website, select Kisan Credit Card, fill and submit the application form; an application reference number is issued, and the bank follows up within 3-4 working days if eligible.
**Offline:** Apply directly at a bank branch.

**Documents required:** Application form, 2 passport-size photos, ID proof (Driving License/Aadhaar/Voter ID/Passport), address proof, landholding proof certified by revenue authorities, details of crops grown with acreage, and security documents for loan limits above ₹1.60 lakh (or ₹3 lakh, as applicable).

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- Do not state a user "is eligible" outright — say the scheme "appears potentially relevant" if they cultivate land (owned, tenant, or share-cropped) or are part of a farmer SHG/JLG, and note that final approval depends on bank assessment and documentation.
- If the user doesn't mention their land/cultivation status, flag this as missing information needed to assess relevance.
