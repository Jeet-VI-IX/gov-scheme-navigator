# PM-USHA (Pradhan Mantri Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan)

**Category:** Higher Education / Skill Development  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Education, Department of Higher Education  
**Official source:** https://pmusha.education.gov.in/pm-usha/#/document

## Objective
- Improve the overall quality of state higher educational institutions by ensuring conformity to prescribed norms and accreditation standards.  
- Implement NEP 2020 reforms in governance, academics, and examinations.  
- Correct regional imbalances in access to higher education, especially in underserved districts.  
- Enhance employability through skilling, vocationalization, and industry-academia linkages.  
- Promote multidisciplinary education, research, and innovation.  
- Support equity and inclusion for SC/ST/OBC, women, minorities, and specially-abled students.  

## Benefits
- Funding support for strengthening universities and colleges (both accredited and unaccredited).  
- Establishment of **Multi-Disciplinary Education and Research Universities (MERUs)**.  
- Creation of **New Model Degree Colleges** in unserved districts.  
- Grants for **Gender Inclusion and Equity Initiatives** in focus districts.  
- Support for ICT-based digital infrastructure, online learning, MOOCs, and virtual labs.  
- Assistance for accreditation of non-accredited institutions and improving NAAC grades.  
- Hostel facilities in remote areas.  
- Research and innovation support, internships, and employability tracking.  

## Eligibility
- Government and government-aided higher education institutions in States/UTs.  
- Priority to institutions in **Focus Districts** (low GER, high SC/ST/OBC/women population, border/LWE/aspirational districts).  
- Institutions must commit to NEP reforms, accreditation, and adoption of Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS) and Academic Bank of Credits (ABC).  

## Exclusions
- Private unaided institutions are not covered under PM-USHA.  
- Institutions unwilling to adopt NEP 2020 reforms or accreditation frameworks.  
- Proposals outside the defined components or exceeding unit cost limits.  

## How to Apply
- States/UTs sign an **MoU with the Ministry of Education** committing to NEP reforms.  
- Institutions submit proposals to State Higher Education Councils (SHEC).  
- States consolidate and forward proposals to the Ministry.  
- Final approval by the **Project Approval Board (PAB)** based on challenge method and performance criteria.  

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- If a user mentions being part of a government or government-aided higher education institution, PM-USHA “appears relevant.”  
- Always highlight **Focus Districts, accreditation support, and NEP 2020 alignment**.  
- Clarify exclusions (private unaided institutions, non-NEP compliant institutions).  
- Stress that PM-USHA is a **funding and reform support scheme**, not a direct student scholarship.
