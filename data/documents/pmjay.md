# PMJAY (Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana)

**Category:** Healthcare
**Ministry:** Ministry of Health and Family Welfare
**Official source:** https://www.pmjay.gov.in

## Objective
Provides financial protection for secondary and tertiary hospitalization to economically vulnerable families, making healthcare cashless and accessible at empanelled hospitals nationwide.

## Benefits
- ₹5 lakh health insurance coverage per family per year (family floater — covers the whole family under one policy).
- Covers ~1,929 medical procedures across 27 specialties (oncology, cardiology, orthopaedics, etc.).
- Cashless treatment at empanelled public and private hospitals.
- Covers pre-hospitalization expenses (up to 3 days) and post-hospitalization expenses (up to 15 days), plus transport costs to reach the hospital.
- Pre-existing diseases covered from day one of enrollment.
- Multiple surgeries in one admission: subsequent surgeries covered at 50% and 25% for the 2nd and 3rd procedure.
- Senior citizens aged 70+ get an additional ₹5 lakh/year top-up coverage exclusively for themselves, regardless of income (added 2024) — those already on CGHS/ECHS/Ayushman CAPF must choose between that and this top-up.

## Eligibility
Based on the Socio-Economic and Caste Census 2011 (SECC-2011):

**Rural households** — any of:
- Live in a house built of unbaked mud/non-durable material with only one room
- No adult male member aged 16-59
- Household has a disabled member and no able-bodied adult
- Scheduled Caste (SC) or Scheduled Tribe (ST) household
- Landless, dependent on manual casual labor
- Legally released bonded labourers, primitive tribal communities, or manual scavenger families

**Urban households** — working in occupations such as: domestic work, street vending, sanitation work, construction labor, transport work (driver/conductor/rickshaw puller), rag picking, home-based artisan/tailoring work, and similar informal-sector jobs.

**Senior citizens 70+**: eligible for the additional top-up regardless of income/socio-economic status.

## Exclusions
Not eligible if the household: owns a two/three/four-wheeler or motorized fishing boat, owns mechanized farming equipment, has a Kisan Credit Card with credit limit ≥ ₹50,000, has a government employee, earns > ₹10,000/month, owns a refrigerator and landline phone, has a pucca (solidly built) house, or owns 5+ acres of agricultural land.

## How to Apply
- Check eligibility via the official PMJAY website (pmjay.gov.in) or by calling the helpline.
- Eligible beneficiaries are issued an Ayushman Card, used to access cashless treatment at any empanelled hospital.
- **Helpline:** 1800-111-565 or 14555

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- Do not state a user "is eligible" outright — SECC-2011-based eligibility can't be self-assessed with certainty from a casual description; say the scheme "appears potentially relevant" if they describe circumstances matching the rural/urban criteria above, and note that final eligibility must be confirmed via the official portal/helpline since it's based on official census records, not self-declaration.
- If the user doesn't mention income, occupation, or living conditions, flag this as missing information needed to assess relevance.
- Note the exclusion criteria are fairly broad (vehicle/land/appliance ownership) — if a farmer persona mentions owning land, check the "5+ acres" and "mechanized farming equipment" exclusions before suggesting this scheme.
