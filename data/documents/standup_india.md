# Stand Up India Scheme

**Category:** Entrepreneurship / Financial Assistance  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Finance  
**Official source:** https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2022/apr/doc20224535601.pdf

## Objective
To promote entrepreneurship among women, Scheduled Castes (SC), and Scheduled Tribes (ST) by facilitating bank loans for greenfield enterprises in manufacturing, services, trading, and activities allied to agriculture.

## Benefits
- Bank loans between **₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore** for eligible entrepreneurs.  
- Coverage across all Scheduled Commercial Bank branches.  
- Margin money requirement reduced to **15% of project cost** (borrower must contribute at least 10%).  
- Collateral-free loans supported by the **Credit Guarantee Fund for Stand-Up India (CGFSI)**.  
- Handholding support via more than **8,000 agencies** (mentorship, skilling, entrepreneurship development).  
- Online portal [www.standupmitra.in](http://www.standupmitra.in) provides loan application guidance and connects borrowers to banks.  

## Eligibility
- SC/ST and/or women entrepreneurs, **above 18 years of age**.  
- Loans only for **greenfield projects** (first-time ventures in manufacturing, services, trading, or allied agriculture).  
- For non-individual enterprises, **51% shareholding and controlling stake** must be held by SC/ST and/or women.  
- Borrowers must **not be in default** to any bank/financial institution.  

## Exclusions
- Male entrepreneurs from non-SC/ST categories are not eligible.  
- Existing businesses (non-greenfield projects) are excluded.  
- Crop loans and land improvement activities (e.g., canals, wells, irrigation) are not covered.  
- Borrowers with prior loan defaults cannot apply.  

## How to Apply
- Apply directly at a **bank branch**, via the **Stand-Up India portal**, or through the **Lead District Manager (LDM)**.  
- Submit project proposal, KYC documents, and margin contribution details.  
- Handholding agencies provide step-by-step support for training, mentorship, and loan application.  

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- If a user mentions being a woman, SC, or ST entrepreneur with a new business idea, this scheme “appears relevant.”  
- Always highlight the **loan range (₹10 lakh–₹1 crore)** and **greenfield project requirement**.  
- Clarify exclusions (non-greenfield, crop loans, male non-SC/ST applicants).  
- Mention that collateral-free loans are backed by CGFSI.
