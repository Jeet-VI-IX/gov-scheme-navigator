# MSSC (Mahila Samman Savings Certificate)

**Category:** Financial Assistance / Women  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Finance  
**Official source:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/mssc

## Objective
To promote savings and financial security among women and girls by offering a safe, government-backed deposit scheme with attractive interest rates.

## Benefits
- Fixed interest rate of **7.5% per annum**, compounded quarterly.  
- Tenure of **2 years** from the date of deposit.  
- Minimum deposit: **₹1,000**.  
- Maximum deposit: **₹2,00,000** (can be in multiple accounts but total across all accounts must not exceed ₹2 lakh).  
- Premature closure allowed in certain cases (death of account holder, extreme compassionate grounds, or after 6 months with reduced interest).  
- Partial withdrawal facility up to **40% of the eligible balance** after one year.  
- Available at post offices and authorized banks nationwide.  

## Eligibility
- Women and girls (account can be opened by a guardian on behalf of a minor girl).  
- Indian citizens only.  
- Deposit must be in multiples of ₹100.  

## Exclusions
- Men cannot open accounts under MSSC.  
- NRIs are not eligible.  
- Deposits exceeding ₹2,00,000 across all MSSC accounts are not permitted.  
- Scheme is not renewable after maturity — fresh deposits must be made for new accounts.  

## How to Apply
- Visit a **post office or authorized bank** with KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN, proof of residence).  
- Fill out the MSSC account opening form and deposit the amount.  
- Passbook issued with details of deposit, maturity, and withdrawal options.  

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- If a user mentions being a woman or guardian of a girl child with savings intent, MSSC “appears relevant.”  
- Always highlight the **₹2 lakh maximum cap** and **women-only restriction**.  
- Clarify that MSSC is a **deposit scheme, not a cash-transfer benefit**.  
- If the user is male or NRI, explicitly flag that MSSC does not apply.
