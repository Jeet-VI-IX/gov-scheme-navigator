# SCSS (Senior Citizen Savings Scheme)

**Category:** Financial Assistance / Senior Citizens  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Finance  
**Official source:** https://www.nsiindia.gov.in/(S(pwuubvrv0uwdr455t3odncyf))/InternalPage.aspx?Id_Pk=134

## Objective
To provide senior citizens with a secure and regular source of income post-retirement through a government-backed savings scheme with attractive interest rates.

## Benefits
- Interest rate: **8.2% per annum** (as of 2023–24), payable quarterly.  
- Deposit limit: minimum **₹1,000**, maximum **₹30 lakh** (in multiples of ₹1,000).  
- Tenure: **5 years**, extendable by **3 years** once.  
- Premature closure allowed after 1 year (with penalty).  
- Interest credited directly to savings account via ECS/post office.  
- Tax benefits under **Section 80C** of the Income Tax Act.  

## Eligibility
- Indian citizens aged **60 years or above**.  
- Retired civilian employees aged **55–60 years** who retired under superannuation/VRS (must invest within 1 month of retirement).  
- Retired defence employees aged **50 years or above**.  
- Joint account allowed only with spouse.  

## Exclusions
- NRIs, PIOs, and HUFs are not eligible.  
- Deposits exceeding ₹30 lakh are not permitted.  
- Premature closure before 1 year not allowed.  
- Interest beyond ₹50,000 per year is subject to TDS if PAN not provided.  

## How to Apply
- Apply at **post offices** or authorized banks with KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN, proof of age, retirement papers if applicable).  
- Fill SCSS application form, deposit amount, and link savings account for interest payout.  
- Passbook issued with deposit and maturity details.  

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- If a user mentions being **60+ or retired (within age rules)**, SCSS “appears relevant.”  
- Always highlight the **₹30 lakh cap** and **5-year tenure**.  
- Clarify exclusions (NRIs, HUFs, under-age applicants).  
- Stress that SCSS is a **deposit scheme with quarterly interest**, not a cash-transfer benefit.
