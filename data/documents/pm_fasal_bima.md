# PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)

**Category:** Agriculture
**Ministry:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare (DAC&FW)
**Official source:** https://pmfby.gov.in

## Objective
Provides crop insurance coverage and financial support to farmers in case notified crops fail due to natural calamities, pests, or diseases — stabilizing farm income and encouraging modern agricultural practices.

## Benefits
- **Yield losses**: Covers loss from natural fire/lightning, storm/hailstorm/cyclone, flood/landslide, drought, and pests/diseases.
- **Prevented sowing**: If adverse weather prevents sowing after a farmer has incurred expenses, indemnity up to 25% of the sum insured.
- **Post-harvest losses**: Coverage up to 14 days after harvest for crops drying in the field, against cyclone/unseasonal rain.
- **Localized calamities**: Covers hailstorm, landslide, and inundation affecting individual farms.
- Premium payable by farmer is capped: 2% of sum insured for Kharif food/oilseed crops, 1.5% for Rabi food/oilseed crops, 5% for annual commercial/horticultural crops (or the actuarial rate, whichever is lower) — the government subsidizes the difference.

## Eligibility
- Any farmer growing a notified crop in a notified area, with insurable interest in that crop.
- **Compulsory** for farmers with an active crop loan/KCC account for the notified crop.
- **Voluntary** for all other farmers, including loanee farmers whose credit limit isn't renewed.

## Exclusions
War and related perils, nuclear risks, riots, malicious damage, theft, crop loss from grazing/damage by domestic or wild animals, and other preventable risks.

## How to Apply
- **Loanee farmers**: Automatically enrolled through their bank at the time of crop loan sanction/renewal; premium is deducted along with the loan.
- **Non-loanee farmers**: Apply voluntarily through a bank branch or insurance intermediary before the state-notified cut-off date (varies by Kharif/Rabi season).
- Claims are assessed based on yield data from Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs) conducted by the state government, and paid out automatically to insured farmers in the notified area if actual yield falls below the threshold yield — no individual claim filing needed for area-level yield losses.

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- Do not state a user "is eligible" outright — say the scheme "appears potentially relevant" if they grow a notified crop in a notified area, and note that actual notified crops/areas vary by state and season, so final confirmation requires checking the official portal.
- If the user doesn't mention what crop they grow, flag this as missing information needed to assess relevance.
- Do not confuse this with WBCIS (Weather Based Crop Insurance Scheme) or UPIS (Unified Package Insurance Scheme) — those are separate, related schemes bundled in the same source document but not the same as PMFBY.
