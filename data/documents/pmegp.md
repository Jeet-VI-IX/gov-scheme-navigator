# PMEGP (Prime Minister’s Employment Generation Programme)

**Category:** Entrepreneurship / Employment  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSME)  
**Official source:** https://pmegp.msme.gov.in/

## Objective
- Generate employment opportunities in rural and urban areas through new micro‑enterprises.  
- Provide sustainable self‑employment to artisans and unemployed youth.  
- Arrest migration of rural youth to urban areas by supporting viable local projects.  

## Benefits
- Credit‑linked subsidy scheme for new micro‑enterprises.  
- Margin Money Subsidy:  
  - **General category:** 15% (urban), 25% (rural).  
  - **Special category (SC/ST/OBC, minorities, women, ex‑servicemen, differently‑abled, NER, aspirational districts, hill/border areas):** 25% (urban), 35% (rural).  
- Beneficiary contribution: 10% (general), 5% (special categories).  
- Maximum project cost:  
  - **Manufacturing sector:** ₹50 lakh.  
  - **Service/Business sector:** ₹20 lakh.  
- Second loan for upgradation: subsidy up to ₹15 lakh (₹20 lakh in NER/hill states).  
- Collateral‑free loans up to ₹10 lakh, priority lending by banks.  
- Entrepreneurship Development Programme (EDP) training and handholding support.  

## Eligibility
- Individuals above 18 years of age.  
- Minimum education: VIII pass for projects above ₹10 lakh (manufacturing) or ₹5 lakh (service).  
- Only new projects sanctioned under PMEGP.  
- Existing units or those already availing subsidy under other schemes are **not eligible**.  
- Mandatory **Udyam Registration** before margin money adjustment.  
- Only one person per family eligible (family = self + spouse).  

## Exclusions
- Projects without capital expenditure.  
- Land cost cannot be included in project cost.  
- Activities in the **negative list** (e.g., liquor, tobacco, certain polluting industries).  
- Trading activities restricted (only Khadi/village industry products or PMEGP/SFURTI cluster products allowed).  
- Crop loans and purely agricultural activities excluded (only allied activities permitted).  
- Existing units under PMRY/REGP or other subsidy schemes not eligible.  

## How to Apply
- Applications submitted **online via PMEGP portal** (manual applications not allowed).  
- Aadhaar authentication required.  
- Upload caste certificate, rural area certificate, project report, education/training certificate, etc.  
- Applications screened by KVIC/KVIB/DIC/Coir Board and forwarded to banks.  
- Banks sanction composite loan (capital + working capital).  
- Margin money subsidy released after physical verification and lock‑in period.  

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- If a user mentions being an unemployed youth, artisan, or entrepreneur seeking to start a new micro‑enterprise, PMEGP “appears relevant.”  
- Always highlight **subsidy rates, project cost limits, and Udyam Registration requirement**.  
- Clarify exclusions (existing units, negative list activities, crop loans).  
- Stress that PMEGP is a **credit‑linked subsidy scheme**, not a direct cash grant.
