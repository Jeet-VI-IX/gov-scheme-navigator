# PMVVY (Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana)

**Category:** Social Security / Senior Citizens  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Finance  
**Official source:** https://pmujjwalayojana.in/pradhan-mantri-vaya-vandana-yojana/

## Objective
To provide senior citizens aged 60 years and above with a safe pension option offering assured returns, financial stability, and protection against market volatility.

## Benefits
- Assured return of **7.40% per annum** (reset annually).  
- Pension payable monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or yearly for **10 years**.  
- Minimum pension: **₹1,000/month**; maximum pension: **₹9,250/month** (₹1,11,000/year for a family).  
- On survival till end of policy term: purchase price + final pension installment refunded.  
- On death during policy term: purchase price refunded to nominee.  
- Loan facility up to **75% of purchase price** after 3 years.  
- Premature exit allowed for critical/terminal illness (refund of 98% of purchase price).  
- GST exempt.  

## Eligibility
- Minimum entry age: **60 years** (completed).  
- No maximum entry age.  
- Policy term: **10 years**.  
- Investment limit: up to **₹15 lakh per senior citizen**.  

## Exclusions
- Not available to individuals below 60 years.  
- Pension ceiling applies to family as a whole (pensioner, spouse, dependents).  
- No renewal after maturity — fresh purchase required.  
- Suicide clause: no exclusion; full purchase price payable.  

## How to Apply
- Purchase through **LIC of India** (offline or online at https://licindia.in).  
- Pay lump-sum purchase price; choose pension mode (monthly/quarterly/half-yearly/yearly).  
- Pension disbursed via NEFT or Aadhaar-enabled payment system.  
- Free-look period: 15 days (30 days for online purchase).  

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- If a user mentions being **60+ with savings intent**, PMVVY “appears relevant.”  
- Always highlight the **₹15 lakh investment cap** and **7.40% assured return**.  
- Clarify that this is a **pension scheme, not a cash-transfer benefit**.  
- If user is below 60, explicitly flag exclusion.
