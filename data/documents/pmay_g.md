# PMAY-G (Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana – Gramin)

**Category:** Housing / Rural Development  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Rural Development  
**Official source:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmay-g

## Objective
To provide financial assistance to rural households living in kutcha or dilapidated houses for construction of pucca houses with basic amenities, ensuring dignity and improved living standards.

## Benefits
- Financial assistance:  
  - **₹1.20 lakh** per house in plain areas.  
  - **₹1.30 lakh** per house in hilly/difficult areas.  
- Additional support:  
  - **₹12,000** for toilets under Swachh Bharat Mission (Gramin).  
  - **90–95 days of wage employment** under MGNREGA for construction.  
- Minimum house size: **25 sq. m** including hygienic cooking space.  
- Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to beneficiary’s bank account.  
- Geo-tagged monitoring via AwaasApp.  

## Eligibility
- Rural households without a pucca house.  
- Priority to SC/ST, minorities, and other disadvantaged groups.  
- Beneficiaries identified through **SECC 2011 data** and verified by Gram Sabha.  
- Must not own a pucca house elsewhere.  

## Exclusions
- Households already owning a pucca house.  
- Families with government employees or above-threshold income.  
- Households with significant assets (motorized vehicles, agricultural equipment, income-tax payers).  

## How to Apply
- Beneficiaries auto-selected from SECC 2011 data, verified by Gram Sabha.  
- Registration done on **AwaasSoft portal** by local authorities.  
- Funds released directly to beneficiary’s bank account via DBT.  
- Construction progress tracked through geo-tagged photos on AwaasApp.  

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- If a user mentions being a rural household without a pucca house, PMAY-G “appears relevant.”  
- Always highlight **₹1.20–1.30 lakh assistance + toilet + MGNREGA support**.  
- Clarify exclusions (pucca house owners, government employees, asset-rich households).  
- Stress that PMAY-G is a **housing construction assistance scheme**, not a loan or subsidy for urban housing.
