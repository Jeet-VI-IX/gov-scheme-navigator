# PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

**Category:** Agriculture
**Ministry:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare
**Official source:** https://pmkisan.gov.in

## Objective
Provides income support to landholding farmers' families to help meet input costs for farming and domestic needs.

## Benefits
₹6,000 per year, paid directly to the farmer's bank account via Direct Benefit Transfer, in three installments of ₹2,000 every 4 months (April–July, August–November, December–March).

## Eligibility
- A "landholder farmer's family" is defined as husband, wife, and minor children who own cultivable land as per state/UT land records.
- Land ownership as per official land records is the basis for eligibility (as of the applicable cut-off date).
- The family's name must appear in the land records of the concerned state/UT.

## Exclusions (not eligible even if landholding)
- Institutional land holders
- Families where a member holds/held a constitutional post, is/was an MP, MLA, Mayor, or District Panchayat Chairperson
- Serving or retired government employees (excluding Multi Tasking Staff / Class IV / Group D)
- Retired pensioners with monthly pension ≥ ₹10,000 (same exclusion for MTS/Class IV/Group D)
- Anyone who paid income tax in the last assessment year
- Registered professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs, architects) actively practicing
- NRIs (for new beneficiary registrations)

## How to Apply
1. Register on the PM-KISAN Portal (pmkisan.gov.in) — either through self-registration or via state/district officials, using Aadhaar, bank account, and land record details.
2. State/UT government verifies and uploads the beneficiary's details.
3. Once verified and Aadhaar-seeded, installments are credited directly to the linked bank account.
