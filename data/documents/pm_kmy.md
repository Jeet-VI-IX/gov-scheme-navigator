# PM-KMY (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maan-Dhan Yojana)

**Category:** Agriculture / Senior Citizens (Pension)
**Ministry:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare (in partnership with LIC)
**Official source:** https://pmkmy.gov.in

## Objective
A voluntary, contributory pension scheme providing social security to Small and Marginal Farmers (SMFs) in old age, when farming becomes physically difficult and many have little or no savings.

## Benefits
- Assured monthly pension of ₹3,000 after reaching age 60.
- Monthly contribution ranges from ₹55 to ₹200 (based on entry age 18-40), matched equally by the Central Government into the pension fund.
- If the subscriber dies before age 60, the spouse can continue the scheme or receive the contributions with interest.
- If the subscriber dies after age 60, the spouse receives 50% of the pension as a family pension.
- Farmers can opt to pay their contribution directly from their PM-KISAN benefit via auto-debit.

## Eligibility
- Small and Marginal Farmers (landholding up to 2 hectares) across all States/UTs.
- Age 18 to 40 at the time of enrollment.

## Exclusions
- Farmers already covered under another statutory social security scheme (NPS, ESIC, EPFO).
- Farmers enrolled in PM-SYM (labourers' pension scheme) or PM-LVM (small traders' pension scheme).
- Institutional landholders.
- Current/former constitutional post holders, ministers, MPs/MLAs, mayors, district panchayat chairpersons.
- Serving or retired government employees (excluding Multi Tasking Staff/Class IV/Group D).
- Anyone who paid income tax in the last assessment year.
- Registered professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs, architects) in active practice.

## How to Apply
1. Visit the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) with Aadhaar card and bank passbook/account details.
2. The CSC operator completes online registration (personal details, spouse/nominee details) and sets up an auto-debit mandate for the monthly contribution.
3. Pay the initial contribution; a PM-KMY Pension Card with a unique account number is issued.
4. Alternative enrollment routes: through State Nodal Officers (SNOs) in the district, or online via the official portal.

**Documents required:** Aadhaar card, bank passbook or account details.

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- Do not state a user "is eligible" outright — say the scheme "appears potentially relevant" if they are a small/marginal farmer aged 18-40, and note that final eligibility depends on confirming no exclusion criteria apply.
- If the user doesn't mention their age or landholding size, flag this as missing information needed to assess relevance.
- This is a good fit for queries about old-age/retirement security for farmers, distinct from PM-KISAN (income support), PMFBY (crop insurance), or KCC (credit).
