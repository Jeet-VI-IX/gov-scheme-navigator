# PMJDY (Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana)

**Category:** Financial Inclusion / Banking  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Finance  
**Official source:** https://pmjdy.gov.in/scheme

## Objective
To ensure universal access to banking facilities, financial literacy, and affordable credit for economically weaker households, thereby promoting financial inclusion across India.

## Benefits
- **Zero-balance savings accounts** with RuPay debit card.  
- **Overdraft facility** up to ₹10,000 (after satisfactory operation of account).  
- **Accidental insurance cover** up to ₹2 lakh (linked to RuPay card).  
- **Life insurance cover** of ₹30,000 (for accounts opened within the first year).  
- Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) of government subsidies into accounts.  
- Access to pension and insurance products.  

## Eligibility
- Any Indian citizen above 10 years of age.  
- Priority to economically weaker households without access to formal banking.  
- Accounts can be opened in any bank branch or through Business Correspondents (Bank Mitras).  

## Exclusions
- Individuals already holding adequate banking facilities.  
- Accounts opened without proper KYC documents (limited functionality until full KYC completed).  
- Overdraft facility not available to minors or accounts without satisfactory operation.  

## How to Apply
- Visit nearest **bank branch** or **Bank Mitra** outlet.  
- Provide Aadhaar card or other valid KYC documents.  
- Fill PMJDY account opening form.  
- Receive RuPay debit card and passbook upon account activation.  

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- If a user mentions being unbanked or lacking access to financial services, PMJDY “appears relevant.”  
- Always highlight **zero-balance account, insurance cover, and overdraft facility**.  
- Clarify exclusions (existing banking access, minors, non-KYC accounts).  
- Stress that PMJDY is a **financial inclusion scheme**, not a loan or subsidy program.
