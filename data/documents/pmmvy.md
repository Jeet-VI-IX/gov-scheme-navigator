# PMMVY (Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana)

**Category:** Healthcare / Women
**Ministry:** Ministry of Women & Child Development
**Official source:** https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2022/jan/doc20221195901.pdf

## Objective
Provides partial compensation for wage loss so a pregnant woman can take adequate rest before and after delivery, and gives a cash incentive to encourage better health-seeking behaviour among Pregnant Women and Lactating Mothers (PW&LM).

## Benefits
- Cash incentive of **₹5,000, paid in 3 instalments**, directly into the beneficiary's Bank/Post Office account:
  - 1st instalment (₹1,000): on early registration of pregnancy.
  - 2nd instalment (₹2,000): after receiving at least one Ante-Natal Check-up (claimable after 6 months of pregnancy).
  - 3rd instalment (₹2,000): after child birth is registered and the child has received the first cycle of BCG, OPV, DPT, and Hepatitis-B (or equivalent/substitute vaccines).
- Beneficiaries additionally receive the Maternity Benefit under **JSY** (Janani Suraksha Yojana) after institutional delivery — so on average, a woman receives about ₹6,000 total combining PMMVY + JSY.
- Also entitled to **Supplementary Nutrition** under Anganwadi Services (a separate Centrally Sponsored Scheme).
- Delivered via PMMVY-CAS, a web-based platform linked to Aadhaar (UIDAI) and PFMS for authentication and to prevent duplicate claims.

## Eligibility
- All Pregnant Women and Lactating Mothers (PW&LM) for their **first living child** in the family, whose pregnancy is on or after 01.01.2017.
- Pregnancy stage/date is determined from the Last Menstrual Period (LMP) date recorded on the Mother and Child Protection (MCP) card.
- Pregnant/lactating Anganwadi Workers (AWWs), Anganwadi Helpers (AWHs), and ASHAs may also avail benefits, subject to meeting the same conditions.
- A beneficiary can receive the full benefit only once. In case of miscarriage/still birth after receiving some instalments, remaining instalments can still be claimed in a future pregnancy (subject to conditions). In case of infant mortality after all instalments have already been received, no further claim is possible.

## Exclusions
- Pregnant Women and Lactating Mothers who are in **regular employment with the Central Government, State Governments, or PSUs**, or who already receive similar benefits under any other law currently in force, are **not eligible**.

## How to Apply
- Implemented through the **Anganwadi Services (ICDS)** platform via the State/UT Women & Child Development Department/Social Welfare Department, or through the Health & Family Welfare Department in States/UTs where it's implemented that way.
- Beneficiaries register and claim instalments through the Anganwadi Centre / health system in their area, tracked on the PMMVY-CAS portal.
- Portal: https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in (per Ministry of Women & Child Development)

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- This FAQ document (Jan 2022) states the benefit applies to the **"first living child"** only. A separate 2025 Ministry press release (on Beti Bachao Beti Padhao's 10th anniversary) mentions PMMVY "now supports a second child if it's a girl" as part of updates under Mission Shakti — this update is **not detailed in this source document** (no revised instalment structure/amount is given here). Do not state specific benefit amounts for a second child/girl child scenario, since they aren't confirmed in this source; instead, if a user mentions a second pregnancy with a girl child, note that an expanded benefit may apply and recommend they verify current details with their local Anganwadi Centre or the official portal.
- If the user doesn't mention which pregnancy/child number this is, flag as missing information — first-child eligibility is a hard criterion in this source.
- If the user mentions government/PSU employment, flag this explicitly as a likely exclusion.
- Complements **PMSMA** (free antenatal checkups) and **JSY** (delivery-linked cash benefit) — a pregnant user's query may match all three; PMMVY specifically covers wage-loss compensation across the pregnancy/early-infancy period via staged instalments, distinct from PMSMA's checkup-day service and JSY's delivery-linked payment.
- Say "appears relevant"/"may be applicable" rather than "eligible" — actual disbursal depends on registration timing and completing each stage's conditions (ANC visit, vaccination cycle), which can't be verified from a single user query.
