# PMS-SC (Post Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Castes)

**Category:** Education
**Ministry:** Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, Department of Social Justice & Empowerment
**Official source:** https://socialjustice.gov.in/writereaddata/UploadFile/PMS_for_SCs_Scheme_Guidelines.pdf

## Objective
Increases the Gross Enrolment Ratio of Scheduled Caste (SC) students in higher education — with a focus on those from the poorest households — by providing financial assistance at the post-matriculation/post-secondary stage so they can complete their education.

## Benefits
- Covers **compulsory non-refundable fees** (tuition, registration, exam, etc., as fixed by the State's Fee Fixation Committee) for the full duration of the course.
- **Academic allowance**, paid yearly, varying by course group and hosteller/day-scholar status:
  - Group 1 (Degree/PG professional courses — Medicine, Engineering, Law, Management, etc.): ₹13,500 (hostellers) / ₹7,000 (day scholars)
  - Group 2 (Other professional courses — Nursing, Pharmacy, Hotel Management, etc.): ₹9,500 / ₹6,500
  - Group 3 (Graduate/PG courses not in Group 1 or 2 — B.A., B.Sc., B.Com, M.A., etc.): ₹6,000 / ₹3,000
  - Group 4 (Post-Class-X non-degree courses — Class XI/XII, diplomas, ITI): ₹4,000 / ₹2,500
  - 10% extra allowance for Divyang (disabled) students.
- **Freeship Card**: lets eligible students take admission without pre-paying tuition/hostel fees; the institution collects payment after the scholarship is disbursed.
- Payment made directly to the student's bank account via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT), preferably through the Aadhaar Payment Bridge.
- Award continues for the full duration of the course, subject to satisfactory academic performance and at least 75% attendance each year, renewed automatically on promotion to the next class.

## Eligibility
- Must be a National of India.
- Must belong to a Scheduled Caste as notified for the State/UT to which the applicant is domiciled/permanently settled.
- Must have passed Matriculation, Higher Secondary, or a higher exam from a recognized University/Board.
- Parental/guardian income from all sources must not exceed **₹2,50,000 per annum** (last financial year); income certificate required once at admission (or once per course, for multi-year courses).
- Must be studying in a recognized institution falling under one of the scheme's 9 categories (Institutions of National Importance, UGC-recognized universities/colleges, deemed universities, recognized private universities, Supreme-Court-fee-regulated private professional institutions, recognized schools for Class XI/XII, State-recognized diploma institutions, NCVT-affiliated vocational institutes, or institutions approved by bodies like NMC/AICTE).
- From 2025-26, participating higher-education institutions must have NAAC/NBA accreditation.
- Correspondence/distance/online course students are also eligible, but receive reimbursement of non-refundable fees only — **no academic allowance**.
- Students admitted through a transparent, merit-based process (advertised applications, ranked merit list) are covered; the State's Fee Fixation/Rationalization Committee must have approved the course fee.

## Exclusions
- Students who, after passing one stage of education, are studying the **same stage** in a different subject (e.g., Intermediate Science after Intermediate Arts, B.Com after B.A., M.A. in one subject after M.A. in another) are **not eligible**.
- Seats filled through arbitrary/non-transparent processes — **management quota, NRI quota, spot admissions** — without following merit criteria are not eligible; fees claimed against such seats are not reimbursed.
- A scholarship holder **cannot hold any other scholarship/stipend** simultaneously — must choose whichever is more beneficial and inform the institution; no PMS-SC payment from the date another scholarship/stipend is accepted. (Free lodging or ad-hoc grants from a State Government or other source, for books/equipment/board expenses, are still permitted alongside this scholarship.)
- No scholarship for studies **outside India**.
- If a student is found to have obtained the scholarship via false statements, it is cancelled, the amount recovered, and the student is **blacklisted and permanently debarred** from any scholarship scheme.

## How to Apply
- Student registers on the relevant scholarship portal (National Scholarship Portal, https://scholarships.gov.in, or the applicable State Scholarship Portal) for a **Freeship Card**, providing Aadhaar number, intended course, and an undertaking to pay institution charges within 7 working days of scholarship receipt.
- On the day of admission, the student applies for the scholarship itself on the portal, and the institution verifies admission details against the Freeship Card the same day.
- Income, caste, and merit details are meant to be auto-verified via linked databases; where this isn't yet in place, scanned documents (photo, academic certificates, caste certificate, income certificate) are uploaded — manual verification is to be completed within 30 days, with "presumed approval" if not completed in that time.
- Renewal students are auto-renewed based on attendance and confirmation of promotion — no fresh application needed.
- Disbursal timelines depend on when the application is received (State share released 15 Aug–28 Feb depending on the application window; Central share follows ~2–6 weeks after).

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- The **₹2.5 lakh/year parental income ceiling** is the current, in-force figure per this guideline document (effective 2020-21 to 2025-26). Note: as of 2026, there are reported government proposals to raise this to ₹4.5 lakh, but that is **not yet in effect** per this official document — do not present ₹4.5 lakh (or any other figure like ₹8 lakh seen in unrelated secondary sources) as the current rule.
- If the user doesn't specify their income, flag as missing information — this is a hard cutoff, not a soft signal.
- If the user doesn't mention which State/UT they are domiciled in, note that eligibility/selection is administered by the applicant's home State/UT, not the state they may currently be studying in.
- Flag as "appears relevant"/"may be applicable" rather than "eligible" — actual sanction also depends on income/caste/merit verification and institutional eligibility (accreditation, AISHE/UDISE code) that this document alone cannot confirm.
- If the user mentions they are already receiving another scholarship/stipend, flag this explicitly — they must choose one or the other, not both.
- This scheme may overlap with a Pre-Matric SC scholarship or other caste/education-based schemes in the knowledge base — note it as one of possibly several options for SC students in education, not the sole match.
