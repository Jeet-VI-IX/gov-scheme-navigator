# PMKVY (Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana)

**Category:** Skill Development / Employment  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship (MSDE)  
**Official source:** https://www.msde.gov.in/offerings/schemes-and-services/details/pradhan-mantri-kaushal-vikas-yojana-4-0-pmkvy-4-0-2021-ITO3ATMtQWa

## Objective
- Provide free, short-duration skill training to youth for wage and self-employment.  
- Make the skilling ecosystem flexible, demand-driven, and technology-enabled.  
- Improve inclusivity by targeting SC, ST, women, and marginalized communities.  
- Promote lifelong skilling through reskilling and upskilling.  
- Focus on new-age skills (AI/ML, AR/VR, Industry 4.0, climate change, green economy).  

## Benefits
- Free training aligned to **National Skills Qualification Framework (NSQF)**.  
- Training types:  
  - **Short-Term Training (STT):** 300–600 hours + On-the-Job Training.  
  - **Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL):** Certification for existing skills, with upskilling options.  
  - **Special Projects:** For marginalized groups, difficult geographies, or future skills.  
- Candidate entitlements: counselling, training, assessment, certification, placement support.  
- Digital tracking via **Skill India Digital (SID)** platform.  
- Industry partnerships for curriculum, OJT, and placements.  

## Eligibility
- Indian nationals with valid Aadhaar.  
- Age group:  
  - **STT & Special Projects:** 15–45 years.  
  - **RPL:** 18–59 years.  
- School/college dropouts, unemployed youth, or candidates seeking reskilling/upskilling.  
- Prior experience required for RPL certification.  

## Exclusions
- Non-Indian nationals.  
- Candidates outside the specified age ranges.  
- Duplicate enrolments across multiple training programmes (must be tracked via SID).  
- Training centres not accredited/affiliated under PMKVY guidelines.  

## How to Apply
- Register via **Skill India Portal / Skill India Digital (SID)**.  
- Counselling and eligibility verification by Training Providers.  
- Training delivered through PMKKs, ITIs, Skill Hubs, industry centres, or accredited private institutions.  
- Assessment by NCVET-recognized agencies; certification issued upon successful completion.  

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- If a user mentions being a youth (15–45) seeking skill training, PMKVY “appears relevant.”  
- Clarify training type (STT, RPL, Special Projects) based on user’s background.  
- Always highlight **free training, certification, and industry relevance**.  
- Flag exclusions (non-Indian, wrong age group, duplicate enrolments).  
- Stress that PMKVY is a **training and certification scheme, not a direct cash benefit**.
