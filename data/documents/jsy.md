# JSY (Janani Suraksha Yojana)

**Category:** Healthcare / Women
**Ministry:** Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (under the National Health Mission)
**Official source:** https://www.govtschemes.in/sites/default/files/2022-03/Janani%20Suraksha%20Yojana.pdf

## Objective
Reduce maternal and infant mortality by promoting institutional (hospital/health-facility) deliveries instead of home births, especially for women from low-income and rural backgrounds.

## Benefits
**Cash assistance for institutional delivery** (amount depends on whether the state is a Low-Performing State/LPS or High-Performing State/HPS):
- LPS: ₹1,400 (rural) or ₹1,000 (urban) to the mother, plus a separate ASHA worker incentive.
- HPS: ₹700 (rural) or ₹600 (urban) to the mother.

**Other benefits:**
- Free antenatal check-ups, essential medicines, and supplements.
- Safe delivery services and emergency obstetric care when needed.
- Postnatal monitoring of mother and newborn, plus guidance on breastfeeding, hygiene, and nutrition.
- Community-level support from ASHA (Accredited Social Health Activist) workers throughout pregnancy and after delivery.

## Eligibility
**Rural areas:**
- Pregnant women from Below Poverty Line (BPL) households.
- Women from Scheduled Caste (SC) or Scheduled Tribe (ST) communities.
- In Low-Performing States, all women delivering in a government health facility qualify (regardless of BPL/SC/ST status).
- Age 19 or above.
- Benefit applies to up to two live births only.

**Urban areas:**
- BPL/SC/ST women delivering in a government or empanelled private facility.
- In Low-Performing States, women qualify irrespective of economic status.

## How to Apply
1. Register the pregnancy at the nearest Anganwadi Centre, with an ASHA worker, or at a government health facility.
2. Provide documents: identity proof, BPL/SC/ST certificate (if applicable), Mother and Child Protection (MCP) Card, and bank account details.
3. Attend regular antenatal check-ups (required to remain eligible).
4. Deliver at a government or accredited private hospital.
5. Cash assistance is transferred directly to the beneficiary's bank account after the facility confirms a safe institutional delivery.

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- Do not state a user "is eligible" outright — say the scheme "appears potentially relevant" if they describe being pregnant/a new mother matching the BPL/SC-ST/rural-urban criteria, and note that the exact cash amount depends on their state's LPS/HPS classification, which should be confirmed locally (e.g., via ASHA worker or health facility).
- If the user doesn't mention which state they're in, income/BPL status, or number of prior live births, flag this as missing information needed to assess relevance (benefit caps at 2 live births).
