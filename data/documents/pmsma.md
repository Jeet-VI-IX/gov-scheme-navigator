# PMSMA (Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan)

**Category:** Healthcare / Women
**Ministry:** Ministry of Health and Family Welfare
**Official source:** https://pmsma.mohfw.gov.in

## Objective
Provides free, quality antenatal (pregnancy) care — especially in the 2nd and 3rd trimesters — to enable early detection of high-risk pregnancies and timely medical intervention, reducing maternal and newborn mortality.

## Benefits
- Free comprehensive antenatal checkup by an Obstetrician/Gynaecologist (or a CEmONC/BEmONC-trained doctor), offered on the 9th of every month at designated government health facilities.
- Free clinical exams, lab tests (blood and urine), ultrasonography, medicines, and counselling on nutrition, birth planning, and safe pregnancy practices.
- Screening for 25 high-risk pregnancy (HRP) factors (e.g., severe anaemia, gestational diabetes, hypertension, twins/multiple pregnancy, teenage or advanced-age first pregnancy, previous C-section, etc.), with the result recorded on the woman's Mother and Child Protection (MCP) card.
- High-risk cases are linked to the nearest First Referral Unit (FRU) for safe delivery and get priority follow-up.
- **Extended PMSMA** (for identified high-risk pregnancies): up to 3 additional monitored antenatal visits, with financial incentives for the beneficiary and the accompanying ASHA worker, plus SMS reminders for registration and follow-up visits.
- No cost to the beneficiary — this is a free service, not a cash-transfer scheme.

## Eligibility
- Pregnant women in their 2nd trimester (13–27 weeks) or 3rd trimester (28 weeks to delivery).
- Indian citizens residing in India, willing to visit a designated government health facility on the 9th of the month.
- Open to all pregnant women in this trimester range — no income, caste, or BPL restriction. Women who have dropped out of regular antenatal care are also specifically encouraged to participate.
- High-risk pregnancy cases receive priority attention.

## How to Apply
- No formal "application" — simply visit a designated government health facility (or an Ayushman Aarogya Mandir) on the 9th of any month during the 2nd or 3rd trimester for the free checkup.
- Follow-up/Extended PMSMA visits are arranged automatically for women flagged as high-risk during the checkup.

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- This scheme is a free service open to essentially all pregnant women in the eligible trimester range — say it "appears relevant" if the user mentions being pregnant (2nd/3rd trimester), without needing to check income or category, since there's no such restriction. There is no "eligible/not eligible" ambiguity to hedge on here the way there is for income-based schemes.
- If the user doesn't mention how many weeks/months pregnant they are, flag this as missing information, since 1st-trimester queries fall outside PMSMA's specific service day but general antenatal care elsewhere would still apply.
- Complements JSY (cash incentive for institutional delivery) — a pregnant user's query may match both; PMSMA covers antenatal care, JSY covers the delivery cash benefit.
