# PMMY (Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana)

**Category:** Entrepreneurship / Financial Assistance  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Finance  
**Official source:** https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2069170&reg=48&lang=2

## Objective
To provide collateral‑free loans to small and micro enterprises, promoting entrepreneurship, financial inclusion, and self‑employment.

## Benefits
- Collateral‑free loans up to **₹20 lakh**.  
- Loan categories:  
  - **Shishu:** up to ₹50,000  
  - **Kishore:** ₹50,000 – ₹5 lakh  
  - **Tarun:** ₹5 lakh – ₹10 lakh  
  - **Tarun Plus:** ₹10 lakh – ₹20 lakh (new category for borrowers who repaid Tarun loans).  
- Loans available through banks, RRBs, NBFCs, MFIs, and Small Finance Banks.  
- Backed by **Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU)**.  
- MUDRA Card (RuPay debit card) for flexible working capital.  
- MUDRA Mitra App for guidance and loan support.  

## Eligibility
- Non‑corporate, non‑farm small/micro enterprises in manufacturing, trading, services, and allied agriculture.  
- Entrepreneurs above 18 years of age.  
- For Tarun Plus: must have successfully repaid earlier Tarun loan.  

## Exclusions
- Large corporates and farm crop loans are not covered.  
- Defaulters to banks/financial institutions are not eligible.  
- Loans cannot be used for speculative or personal consumption purposes.  

## How to Apply
- Approach a **bank branch, NBFC, or MFI**.  
- Submit project proposal, KYC documents, and loan application form.  
- Loan sanctioned under appropriate category (Shishu, Kishore, Tarun, Tarun Plus).  

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- If a user mentions being a small entrepreneur needing funds, PMMY “appears relevant.”  
- Always highlight the **loan category ranges** and **collateral‑free nature**.  
- Clarify exclusions (large corporates, farm crop loans, defaulters).  
- Stress that PMMY is a **loan scheme, not a grant or subsidy**.
