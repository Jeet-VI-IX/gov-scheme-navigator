# PMAY-U (Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana – Urban)

**Category:** Housing / Urban Development  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs  
**Official source:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmay-u

## Objective
To provide affordable housing to urban poor, slum dwellers, and economically weaker sections (EWS) by 2022, ensuring “Housing for All” through financial assistance and subsidies.

## Benefits
- **Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS):** Interest subsidy on home loans for EWS, LIG, MIG‑I, and MIG‑II categories.  
- **In‑situ Slum Redevelopment (ISSR):** Redevelopment of slums with private participation.  
- **Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP):** Financial assistance to EWS families for houses built in partnership with public/private sectors.  
- **Beneficiary‑Led Construction (BLC):** Assistance up to **₹1.5 lakh** per EWS family for new construction or enhancement of existing houses.  
- Houses must have basic civic amenities (water, sanitation, electricity).  

## Eligibility
- Urban households without a pucca house anywhere in India.  
- Priority to EWS (annual income ≤ ₹3 lakh) and LIG (₹3–6 lakh).  
- MIG‑I (₹6–12 lakh) and MIG‑II (₹12–18 lakh) eligible under CLSS component.  
- Beneficiary family includes husband, wife, unmarried sons/daughters.  

## Exclusions
- Families owning a pucca house anywhere in India.  
- Households not falling under defined income categories.  
- Applicants already availing benefits under other housing schemes.  

## How to Apply
- Apply online via **PMAY‑U portal** or through Common Service Centres (CSCs).  
- Submit Aadhaar, income proof, and family details.  
- Selection and verification by Urban Local Bodies (ULBs).  
- Subsidy/assistance credited directly to beneficiary’s bank account or adjusted in loan account.  

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- If a user mentions being an urban household without a pucca house, PMAY‑U “appears relevant.”  
- Always highlight **CLSS interest subsidy** and **₹1.5 lakh assistance for EWS**.  
- Clarify exclusions (pucca house owners, ineligible income groups).  
- Stress that PMAY‑U is a **housing assistance scheme for urban areas**, distinct from PMAY‑G (rural).
