# IGNOAPS (Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme)

**Category:** Social Security / Senior Citizens  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Rural Development  
**Official source:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/nsap-ignoaps

## Objective
To provide social security and financial assistance to elderly citizens living below the poverty line, ensuring basic sustenance and dignity in old age.

## Benefits
- Monthly pension of **₹200 per beneficiary** (from Central Government funds).  
- States/UTs may contribute additional amounts, often raising the pension to **₹500–₹1,000 per month** depending on local provisions.  
- Direct transfer to beneficiary’s bank/post office account under DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer).  

## Eligibility
- Age **60 years and above**.  
- Belonging to a **Below Poverty Line (BPL) household**.  
- Must be a resident of India.  

## Exclusions
- Individuals below 60 years of age.  
- Non-BPL households.  
- Pension not available to government employees or those already receiving other social security pensions.  

## How to Apply
- Apply through the **Gram Panchayat / Block Development Office** in rural areas or the **Municipal office** in urban areas.  
- Submit proof of age, residence, and BPL status.  
- Pension is sanctioned and disbursed through the State/UT Social Welfare Department.  

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- If a user mentions being **60+ and from a BPL household**, IGNOAPS “appears relevant.”  
- Always clarify that the **base pension is ₹200/month**, but states may top it up.  
- If the user is under 60 or not BPL, explicitly flag that IGNOAPS does not apply.  
- Highlight that this is a **social security pension**, not a one-time grant.
