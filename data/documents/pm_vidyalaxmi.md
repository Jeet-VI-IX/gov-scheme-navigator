# PM-Vidyalaxmi (Pradhan Mantri Vidyalaxmi Scheme)

**Category:** Education / Financial Assistance
**Ministry:** Ministry of Education, Department of Higher Education
**Official source:** https://www.dohe-education.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/10/18ce1793a6e249c2cdaccc7787739305.pdf

## Objective
Ensures financial constraints don't prevent meritorious students from pursuing quality higher education, by driving collateral-free, guarantor-free education loans (with credit guarantee and interest subvention) for admission into India's top-ranked Higher Educational Institutions (QHEIs).

## Benefits
- **Collateral-free, guarantor-free education loan** for admission (on own merit) into a Quality Higher Education Institution (QHEI) — no cap on loan amount; it depends on course fee plus other costs (hostel, mess, laptop, living expenses), varying by institute.
- **75% credit guarantee** from the Government of India on loan amounts up to ₹7.5 lakh, regardless of family income, so banks can extend more loans.
- **3% interest subvention** on education loans up to ₹10 lakh (on the principal amount, even if the total loan is larger) during the moratorium period (course period + 1 year), for students with annual family income up to ₹8 lakh.
- Students with annual family income up to ₹4.5 lakh, pursuing technical/professional courses at NAAC/NBA-accredited institutions, get **100% interest subvention** instead, under the related PM-USP CSIS scheme (not PM-Vidyalaxmi itself) — PM-Vidyalaxmi's 3% subvention applies for other courses in this income band, and for all QHEI courses at ₹4.5–8 lakh income.
- Interest rate on the loan is capped at the bank's Externally Benchmarked Lending Rate (EBLR) + 0.5%, and must be lower than the bank's rate for non-PM-Vidyalaxmi education loans.
- Repayment period: up to 15 years, excluding the moratorium period.
- Simple, fully digital 2-page application process via a unified portal.
- Only 1 lakh interest-subvention slots are available per year nationally (distributed state-wise by population); the base loan/credit-guarantee benefit itself has no cap on number of beneficiaries.

## Eligibility
- Must secure admission **on own merit** (via open competitive exam or merit-based admission) to a **Quality Higher Education Institution (QHEI)** — one of the ~860 institutions on DHE's annual list (top NIRF-ranked HEIs, top state-government HEIs, and central-government HEIs). The list updates yearly, but a student already selected keeps support even if their institute drops off a later list.
- Must be enrolled in a Graduation/Post-Graduation degree or diploma course at the QHEI.
- **Loan eligibility**: open to students of all family income groups.
- **3% interest subvention eligibility**: annual family income (student + father + mother + spouse, where applicable) up to ₹8 lakh.
- Interest subvention and credit guarantee are admissible only **once** per student, for either UG, PG, or an integrated course (not repeatable across levels).
- Only Indian institutions on the QHEI list qualify — study must be in India.

## Exclusions
- Students admitted through **management quota** (or similar quota) are **not eligible** for the loan.
- Indian campuses of foreign institutions, foreign campuses of Indian institutions, and foreign institutions themselves are **not covered**.
- A student already availing **any other Central/State Government scholarship, interest subvention, or fee reimbursement scheme** is not eligible for PM-Vidyalaxmi benefits (de-duplication is enforced via Aadhaar/PFMS); they must choose one scheme.
- Interest subvention and credit guarantee are **not available** if the student discontinues the course midstream or is expelled on disciplinary/academic grounds — **except** if discontinuation is on medical grounds, with documentation.
- If a student's declaration is later found false, they are debarred from future benefits under any government scheme, subject to recovery of amounts paid and possible legal action.

## How to Apply
- Apply through the unified **PM-Vidyalaxmi portal** (administered by DHE, developed/maintained by Canara Bank as nodal bank) — a simple 2-page digital application.
- Student declares annual family income at the time of loan application; no income certificate is required upfront.
- Once the loan is sanctioned and disbursed by the bank, students with declared income under ₹8 lakh are shortlisted and invited (within a stipulated period) to apply separately for the interest subvention on the same portal.
- Required documents for interest subvention: KYC (Aadhaar, PAN, address proof), prior mark sheets, entrance exam result, institution offer letter with fee structure, and income proof from a designated public authority (or a certificate from the QHEI if already verified at admission).
- A self-declaration (Annexure 7 format) confirming the student isn't receiving any other government scholarship/interest subvention is required; the bank must also certify the same (Annexure 8 format).
- Interest subvention amount is credited to a digital e-voucher/wallet, which the student must redeem toward their loan account within 3 months, or it lapses for that year.
- From the 2nd year onward, continued subvention depends on satisfactory academic performance, tracked via APAAR or self-attested result uploads.

## Notes for the assistant (grounding constraints)
- This is a **loan-based** scheme, not a grant/scholarship — flag this distinction clearly to the user, since PMS-SC/NMMSS-type schemes are direct financial assistance while this creates loan liability (albeit with subsidized interest).
- If the user doesn't specify their institute/college, flag as missing information — eligibility is entirely dependent on the institute being on the official QHEI list (~860 institutions), which cannot be verified from this document alone; note that the assistant cannot confirm whether a specific college is on the list without checking the current annual list.
- If the user doesn't mention family income, flag as missing information — it determines only the *interest subvention* tier (not loan eligibility itself, which has no income bar).
- Say "may be applicable"/"appears relevant" rather than "eligible" — even meeting income and QHEI criteria doesn't guarantee interest subvention, since only 1 lakh subvention slots exist per year nationally and selection uses a sequential priority method (state quota → government HEI → technical/professional course → government/rural schooling background → gender), which this document alone can't predict for an individual applicant.
- If the user mentions already having another scholarship or interest subvention, flag this explicitly — they can't combine benefits and must choose one.
- This scheme overlaps with **PM-USP CSIS** (full interest subvention for ≤₹4.5 lakh income, technical/professional courses at NAAC/NBA-accredited HEIs) — if the user's income is ≤₹4.5 lakh and they're in a technical/professional course, note both schemes as relevant, since CSIS may offer a stronger benefit (100% vs 3% subvention).
